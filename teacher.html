Present slides

A · PowerPoint with animationsRecommended
  1. Open your PowerPoint and start the slide show (F5).
  2. Come back to this page (Alt + Tab), type the title and press Share my PowerPoint.
  3. Choose the Window tab → PowerPoint Slide Show → Share.
  4. Students scan the QR code. Go back to PowerPoint (Alt + Tab) and present as normal. Every click, animation and video plays on their iPads.

Use Chrome or Edge on your computer. The iPads must be on the same Wi-Fi as the computer. Students who leave turn red, the same as in a test.

B · Or upload the slides as a PDF (sharp pictures, but no animations)

1 · Choose your slides
PowerPoint: File → Export → Create PDF/XPS → Publish. Then choose that PDF here.
Google Slides, Keynote, Canva: download as PDF.
Pictures (PNG, JPG) work too. Pick several and each one becomes a slide.

Students scan the QR code and see the slide you are on. If they leave the page, their screen locks and their name turns red, the same as in a test. Animations and videos inside slides don't come across.
